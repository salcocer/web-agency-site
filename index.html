FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a website take?

Most sites launch in 3 to 6 weeks. The Essential package is usually live in about 3 weeks; booking and e-commerce sites take closer to 6.

Will I be able to edit the website myself?

Yes. We build on WordPress or Shopify and give you a short training session, so you can change text, photos and prices without us.

Do you make websites in Thai and English?

Yes. Our Signature package includes both languages, and we can add Thai to any other package.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do, from day one. The domain, hosting account and code are registered in your name.

Can you take over an existing website?

Yes. We can redesign it, or simply move it onto our Care plan for hosting, updates and support.
